In this task, the objective is to familiarize yourself with time series and their internal structure. To complete the task:

- Download the random time series generator (Moodle).
- Generate typical time series for two domains (examples could be Tides, Seismography, Stock market value of a company from December 2019 to February 2022, Number of reservations at a holiday resort from January 2022 to September 2024, etc.)

Submit a brief report (2-3 pages) that includes:

- Description of each domain and characteristics of their times series
- Parameters used to simulate the time series in the app
- Images of the two types of time series (real and simicled)
- ( Calculate the average distance between series from different domains and between series from the same domain)
- Conclusions (how well the series were simimed, pros and cons of the tool, etc.)

Just one element of the group delivers the report, including the names of all group members in the report!
